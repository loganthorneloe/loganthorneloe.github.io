Core Values

  • • Software and technology are user problems. Software designed around the user experience succeeds. Anything else is an uphill battle. This is even more important when building machine learning systems.
  • • The future of AI requires incredible engineers. The impact of AI relies on how well we can apply it. Additionally, we don't truly understand that which we can't build with.
  • • You can just do things, but don't do too much. It's better to take a step back and ensure you're headed in the right direction.
  • • Always be yourself. In work, life, and relationships, everything tends to work out better when you do. Also, everything's more fun with a bit of whimsy.
