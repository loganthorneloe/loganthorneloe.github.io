What I believe

  1. Pragmatic optimism. Believe things can get better, be honest about what's hard, and do the work to make progress.

  2. Software and technology are user problems. Software designed around the user experience succeeds. Anything else is an uphill battle. This is even more important when building machine learning systems.

  3. You can just do things, but don't do too much. It's better to take a step back and ensure you're headed in the right direction.

  4. Always be yourself. In work, life, and relationships, everything tends to work out better when you do. Also, everything's more fun with a bit of whimsy.

  5. Ask for help. The best way to get better at something is to learn from others. Most people will be willing to help.
